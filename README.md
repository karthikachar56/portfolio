## 🌟 Overview

Modern, responsive executive portfolio showcasing engineering leadership, entrepreneurial milestones with **EETIRP PVT LTD**, and high-impact full-stack and AI deployments.

### Key Architecture & Features
- **Responsive 12-Column Bento Grid**: Clean dimensions for executive profile, academic distinction, quick communications with 1-click clipboard copy, and categorized technical tooling.
- **Founder Journey Timeline**: 4-phase milestone trajectory highlighting the inception, challenges, deployments, and future roadmap of EETIRP.
- **EETIRP Enterprise Spotlight Card**: Dark glassmorphic gradient card with ambient glow and live platform links.
- **Dynamic Animation Suite**: 
  - Shimmering iridescent gradient typography
  - Floating ambient glow orbs and breathing profile badge animation
  - Animated live stat counter (`requestAnimationFrame` with ease-out cubic)
  - Top scroll reading progress indicator
  - Interactive mouse spotlight reflection following card hover
  - Scroll-driven staggered reveal animations (`IntersectionObserver`)
- **Accessibility & SEO**: Semantic HTML5, preconnected Google Fonts (Outfit & Plus Jakarta Sans), Open Graph & Twitter Cards, and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` support.
