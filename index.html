Trajectory

Founder Journey

How EETIRP evolved from an observed campus divide into an enterprise AI ecosystem

Phase 01 • Genesis

Why I Co-Founded EETIRP

I noticed an alarming gap early in college: while top companies demand practical exposure to microservices, modern APIs, and clean architecture, academic courses focus almost purely on abstract theory. I started EETIRP to build an interactive, hands-on student ecosystem.

Phase 02 • Engineering

Overcoming Bottlenecks

Launching a venture as a student in Bengaluru is demanding. We engineered high-volume API registration loops, scaled candidate assessment databases under intense test traffic, and resolved latency bottlenecks by standardizing backend routes on modern cloud architectures.

Phase 03 • Traction

Key Milestones

Successfully bootstrapped the flagship student dashboard system, launched placement bootcamps in major institutions, and deployed multiple core apps. Our AI-powered placement evaluators have guided over 100+ students toward technical mastery.

Phase 04 • Vision

Nationwide Horizon

Our mission for the upcoming 12 months is expanding the EETIRP ecosystem with nationwide university bootcamps, automated candidate tracking networks, and native AI interview simulators directly partnering with recruiters.
